# ArchXQ IT Lite — architecture by phases for IngeTrazo

ArchXQ IT turns [IngeTrazo](https://ingetrazo.com) (the free, open-source
3D modeler) into an architecture workflow: you go through the project in
phases — Project, Terrain, Structure, Walls, Openings, Roof, Documentation —
and every element is drawn with real sizes, on real levels.

**Lite** is free and open source (GPL-3.0). It draws:

- **Project & levels** — floors, basements, floor heights
- **Terrain** — the plot (rectangle, point by point, from survey points),
  sloped plots, setbacks, **excavations and fills** with ramps and
  per-corner depths
- **Walls** — straight, curved and round; outside / centre / inside
  alignment; walls join by themselves
- **Columns** — stamp, draw, a row (by count or spacing) or a grid;
  9 insertion points; faces snap to walls, the plot and other columns

**ArchXQ IT Pro** adds beams, slabs, footings, doors and windows, roofs,
rooms, drawings and sheets, sections, PDF and DXF export, and walls from a
DXF plan — plus updates for every new IngeTrazo version.
→ **Get Pro:** https://www.xq.com.br/archxq-it

A file made with the Pro opens in the Lite with everything visible and
kept: the Lite never removes what it cannot edit.

## Install

1. Download the zip and unzip it: you get a folder `archxq_it`. That whole
   folder is the extension — do not take the files out of it.
2. In IngeTrazo: **Extensions ▸ Open add-ons folder**, and copy the
   `archxq_it` folder there.
3. Close and open IngeTrazo again. Turn ArchXQ on with the **⏻ ArchXQ**
   switch at the top left of the 3D view.

Upgrading to the Pro: replace the `archxq_it` folder with the Pro's and
restart. Your files stay as they are.
